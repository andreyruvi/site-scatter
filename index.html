Recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all support it; a very old machine, a remote desktop session or hardware acceleration turned off are the usual reasons it is missing.

The site

The ground is generated, not surveyed. Where you have levels, use them: set the relief to zero and enter your mean fall, so the slope limits are checked against a gradient you actually measured.

Rebuilding the ground keeps the planting where it is in plan, so some of it may end up on slopes it would not have been painted on. The schedule will say which.

What was checked

Site Scatter is original work by Duong L. — no dependencies, no tracking, no server. Source and licence on GitHub.

The planting geometry is an indicative silhouette, not a plant model, and the ground is generated rather than surveyed. The tool checks the numbers you give it against each other; it does not know your planning policy, your soil or your climate, and it does not check them. A clean schedule is not an approval.
